Questions, answered

Is it really free?

Yes — 100% free, forever, with no ads and no hidden costs. It's open source and built as a public-good product by MNB Research.

Do I need to install anything?

No. Open the link in any browser and use it instantly. You can optionally "Add to Home Screen" to use it like an app, and there's a full Android build too.

Is my data safe?

Completely. Everything lives on your device, encrypted where sensitive (AES-256). There are no servers, accounts or tracking — location is only shared with family when you switch it on.

Does it work without internet?

Core features — SOS flow, first-aid, evidence vault, fake call, alarm — work offline. Live maps and family tracking need a connection.
