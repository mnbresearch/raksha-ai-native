Good to know

Questions, answered

Is it really free?

Yes — 100% free, forever, no ads and no hidden costs. Raksha AI is open source, built as a public-good product by MNB Research.

Do I need to install anything?

No. Open the link in any browser and use it instantly. You can "Add to Home Screen" to run it like an app, and there's a free Android build too.

Is my data safe?

Completely. Everything lives on your device, encrypted where sensitive (AES-256). No servers, accounts or tracking — location is shared with family only when you turn it on.

Does it work without internet?

Core features — the SOS flow, first-aid, evidence vault, fake call, alarm, scam shield — work fully offline. Live maps and family tracking need a connection.

Absolutely. MNB Research offers branded rollouts, bulk onboarding and partnerships — reach out below.
