🛡️ Raksha AI — User Guide

In any real emergency, call 112 first.

Raksha AI is your safety companion — it assists and speeds up help, but it never replaces emergency services.

⚡ 60-second setup (do this once)

  1. Install: open the app in Chrome → menu (⋮) → Add to Home Screen.
  2. Allow permissions when asked: Location, Microphone, Notifications, Camera. Choose “Allow all the time” for location on the Android app.
  3. Add your circle: More → Trusted Circle → add 2–3 people (or Import from phone contacts). Nothing works without this.
  4. Run diagnostics: Settings → 🩺 Run full diagnostics → aim for 100%.
  5. Practice once: Quick Tools → SOS Practice Drill.

🚨 How to trigger SOS (6 ways)

  • Hold the red SOS button (SOS tab) for 1.5 seconds.
  • Floating SOS bubble — the red circle on every screen; hold it.
  • Shake your phone hard 3 times (enable in Quick Tools).
  • Say your secret phrase or “help me” / “bachao” (enable Voice Guardian).
  • Tap the Guardian orb 5 times fast on the Home screen.
  • Silent SOS: hold the clock (top-right) 3 seconds — nothing shows on screen, but recording starts and family/volunteers are alerted.

What happens: a 15-second check-in appears → if you don’t respond, the app records audio, locks your GPS, messages your circle (and Telegram if set up), and offers a one-tap call to your contact or 112.

💡 Turn on “Require PIN to stop emergency” (Settings) so an attacker can’t cancel your SOS.

🛰️ Family Live — see each other in real time

  1. Go to More → Trusted Circle → Family Live.
  2. Pick a long, private family code (e.g. sharma-tigers-9481) and enter your name.
  3. Turn on “Go live with my family.”
  4. Share the code — family members enter the same code and go live too.

No app? No problem. Send relatives the tracker link — it opens in any browser:
…/track.html?fam=YOURCODE

💡 For alerts that reach phones even when apps are closed, set up Telegram Alerts (instructions in the Circle tab — 5 minutes, free).

🚶‍♀️ Before you walk or travel

  • Walk With Me (Home) — keep your thumb on the button; release it and don’t return → help is called.
  • AI Companion Call — a voice that talks with you and listens for “help me”.
  • Trip Mode — set a destination (or tap a saved zone like → Home); the Guardian watches your route and checks on you if it deviates.
  • Cab Logger — log the plate before boarding; instantly shared with family.
  • Safety Timer — “I should be safe in 30 min”; forget to tap safe → it escalates.
  • 🌙 Night Shield — one switch arms fall + scream + crash detection.

🔐 Safe Space (for anyone facing abuse)

A hidden, encrypted vault for evidence:

  1. Set a Safe Space PIN (Settings). This encrypts everything with AES-256.
  2. Save photos, voice notes, and dated incident notes.
  3. Export a court-ready PDF (with photos) for a lawyer or police.
  4. Quick Exit instantly disguises the app as a calculator (type your PIN + = to return).
  5. Set a Duress PIN — if forced to open the vault, it shows an empty decoy.
⚠️ If you forget your PIN, vault items cannot be recovered — that’s real security. In immediate danger: 112. Women helpline: 181 / 1091.

🕵️ Scam Shield

Paste any suspicious SMS/WhatsApp message (or use 📋 Paste, or share it to Raksha from another app) → it checks 28 Indian fraud patterns on your phone. If you’ve lost money, call 1930 within 24 hours.

🧠 Guardian Brain & Assistant

Brain shows a live threat meter and an honest log of why it thinks what it thinks — and learns your normal places/hours on-device. Assistant answers plain questions (“someone is following me”, “I got a KYC message”) with exact steps and one-tap actions. Works offline.

❓ Troubleshooting

  • Old version showing: close the app fully and reopen twice.
  • GPS stuck on “waiting”: allow Location in site settings; stand near a window.
  • Family member not appearing: same exact code on both; app must be open (web) — the Android app works in the background.
  • Android kills it overnight: Settings → Apps → Raksha AI → Battery → Unrestricted (+ Autostart on Xiaomi/Oppo/Vivo).
  • Voice features silent: use Chrome on Android; iPhone support is limited.
